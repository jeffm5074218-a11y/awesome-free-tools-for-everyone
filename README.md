**The best free AI and creative tools, explained in plain words for regular folks.** No jargon, no paid upsells, nothing that needs a credit card to get started.

## AI models

- **[Llama](https://www.llama.com)**: Meta's free AI model. One of the most popular to run yourself.
- **[Mistral](https://mistral.ai)**: Fast, small AI models from a French company. Great on a home computer.
- **[Gemma](https://ai.google.dev/gemma)**: Google's free, lightweight AI models.
- **[Qwen](https://huggingface.co/Qwen)**: Free AI models from Alibaba, good at many languages and coding.
- **[DeepSeek](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai)**: Free AI models known for strong reasoning and math.
- **[Phi](https://huggingface.co/microsoft)**: Microsoft's tiny AI models that run on regular laptops.

## AI tools

- **[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co)**: The big library of free AI models. Try thousands right in your browser.
- **[Ollama](https://ollama.com)**: Run free AI models on your own computer with one download.
- **[LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai)**: Point-and-click app for chatting with free AI models offline.
- **[Jan](https://jan.ai)**: A free, private AI chat app that runs on your own computer.
- **[OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai)**: One place to try lots of AI models, including free ones.

## Images & art

- **[ComfyUI](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI)**: Make AI pictures with Stable Diffusion by connecting boxes together.
- **[Fooocus](https://github.com/lllyasviel/Fooocus)**: Simple, free AI image maker. Type what you want and go.
- **[Upscayl](https://upscayl.org)**: Free AI upscaler that makes small or blurry pictures sharp.
- **[Photopea](https://www.photopea.com)**: A Photoshop-style photo editor that runs free in your browser.
- **[GIMP](https://www.gimp.org)**: Free photo editor, like Photoshop.
- **[Krita](https://krita.org)**: Free digital painting and drawing.
- **[Inkscape](https://inkscape.org)**: Make logos, stickers and shirt designs that stay sharp at any size.

## Voice & audio

- **[Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper)**: Turns speech and recordings into written text.
- **[Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper)**: Free text-to-speech voices that run on your own computer.
- **[Ultimate Vocal Remover](https://github.com/Anjok07/ultimatevocalremovergui)**: Pull the singing out of a song, or the music out from under it.
- **[Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org)**: Record and clean up audio, voiceovers and podcasts.
- **[LMMS](https://lmms.io)**: Make your own music and beats for free.
- **[MuseScore](https://musescore.org)**: Write and play sheet music.

## Writing

- **[LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org)**: Free grammar and spelling checker for your writing.
- **[Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com)**: Paste your writing in and it shows you how to make it clearer.
- **[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md)**: Keep notes and ideas linked together. Free for personal use.
- **[LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org)**: Free Word, Excel and PowerPoint replacement.

## Video

- **[DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)**: Pro-level video editing. The free version does almost everything.
- **[Shotcut](https://shotcut.org)**: Simple, free video editor for everyday projects.
- **[Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org)**: Free video editor with lots of effects and transitions.
- **[OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com)**: Record your screen or stream live to YouTube.
- **[HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr)**: Shrink and convert videos to play on any device.

## Make & build

- **[Blender](https://www.blender.org)**: Full 3D modeling and animation. Make models for your printer, or whole cartoons.
- **[OrcaSlicer](https://github.com/SoftFever/OrcaSlicer)**: Free slicer for 3D printers, Bambu included.
- **[FreeCAD](https://www.freecad.org)**: Design real parts and builds with exact measurements.
- **[Tinkercad](https://www.tinkercad.com)**: The easiest way to start 3D design, right in your browser.

## Everyday

- **[VLC](https://www.videolan.org)**: Plays just about any video or music file.
- **[7-Zip](https://www.7-zip.org)**: Opens and makes zip files and other archives.
- **[Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com)**: Free password manager that remembers all your logins.
- **[Notepad++](https://notepad-plus-plus.org)**: A better notepad for writing and fixing code.

## Free AI with no credit card (for builders)

Want to put free AI in your own app or website? See **[free-ai-anywhere](https://github.com/jeffm5074218-a11y/free-ai-anywhere)**: one small file that uses Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter and Cloudflare free tiers, none of which need a card.
